PETSHIFT / PET CATALOGUE
Snapshot date: 13 November 2026

Primary directory: https://www.playadopt.me/discover/pets
Sitemap cross-check: https://www.playadopt.me/sitemap.xml

The published directory returned 843 entries: 801 pets and 42 eggs.
All 801 non-egg entries are available in the Settings picker.
Every pet-directory route in the sitemap was represented in that data.
Names, rarities, and origins follow the official directory.

Artwork uses the public image asset IDs published with directory entries.
761 pet thumbnails are bundled from Roblox's public thumbnail service.
40 entries use a built-in PetShift icon where no usable thumbnail returned.
All 801 pets can be selected regardless of artwork availability.
Normal, Neon, and Mega Neon choices change the pet labels and display glow;
the bundled thumbnail shows the base pet design.

These files contain an offline catalogue. Future game additions require
an updated catalogue. The application makes no network requests.

PetShift is independent. Roblox and Adopt Me assets remain the property
of their respective owners. This interface is not an official game tool.
